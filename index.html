TimeBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Welcome to TimeBridge. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the TimeBridge mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

TimeBridge collects minimal information necessary to provide our fare and route calculation services.

a) Information You Provide

  • Full Name — Used for personalizing trip summaries and notifications.
  • Email Address — Used for app functionality and account management.
  • Location Search Terms — Pickup and dropoff locations you type for fare calculation.
  • Distance (optional) — If manually entered by you.

b) Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Push Token — Generated by Expo if you grant notification permission. Used only to send you trip notifications.
  • App Preferences — Currency, distance unit, duration unit, and map theme settings stored locally on your device.

c) Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your precise GPS location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, files, or camera.
  • We do not collect biometric data — fingerprint authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system and never reaches our servers.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across other apps.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Calculate distance, duration, and fare for your trips.
  • Display your route on an interactive map.
  • Send optional trip notifications (if you allow).
  • Personalize your experience with saved preferences.
  • Improve app functionality and fix issues.

3. Third-Party Services

TimeBridge uses the following third-party services to provide core functionality. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — Geocoding service. Receives the search terms you type to find location coordinates. Privacy Policy
  • OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine) — Routing service. Receives pickup and dropoff coordinates to calculate driving routes. Website
  • Expo Push Notifications — Delivery of optional notifications. Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap / CartoCDN — Map tile providers for displaying maps. Privacy Policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your name, email, and preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted AsyncStorage.
  • Data transmission to third-party services uses HTTPS encryption.
  • We do not maintain user accounts on our servers.
  • We retain no personal data on our own servers.

5. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. To delete your data:

  • On-device: Uninstall the app or clear app data from your device settings. This removes all stored information immediately.
  • By request: Email us at contact@timebridge.app and we will process your deletion request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

TimeBridge is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for notifications at any time via device settings.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of TimeBridge after changes means you accept the updated policy.
